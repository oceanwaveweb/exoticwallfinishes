## Detailed Evaluation Pillars & Strategic Opportunities for Exotic Wall Finishes

### 1. Schema & Structured Data Blueprint (Technical Opportunity)
Most competitors exhibit severe schema gaps, relying only on default theme metadata. Exotic Wall Finishes will implement an industry-leading schema hierarchy:

- **`Product` & `OfferCatalog` Schema:** Rich snippets with exact pricing, availability, aggregate rating, GTINs, and material specs for all lime plasters, trowels, spatulas, and metallic pastes.
- **`Service` & `LocalBusiness` Schema:** Geo-targeted schema for B2B/B2C application services covering key metro areas (NYC, Miami, LA, Chicago, Dallas, Scottsdale, London).
- **`HowTo` & `VideoObject` Schema:** Embedded step-by-step application walkthroughs (e.g., *How to Apply Grassello di Calce*, *Burnishing Venetian Plaster to a Mirror Finish*).
- **`FAQPage` Schema:** Comprehensive structured answers on pricing, coverage rates, substrate preparation, waxing maintenance, and fire/VOC ratings.

### 2. High-Intent Content Architecture & Topic Cluster Gaps
Competitors either focus strictly on selling raw materials or operating as silent local contractor portfolios. Exotic Wall Finishes will bridge this divide by launching 4 dedicated content hubs:

1. **The Plaster & Finish Catalog (`/products/...`):** Granular pages for Grassello, Marmorino (Fine, Coarse, Carrara), Calce Rasata, Intonachino, Metaline, Velvet Rust, and Microcement.
2. **Master Tool Store (`/tools/...`):** Categorized by blade geometry, steel flexibility, beveling grade, and handle ergonomics (Pavan style, Japanese trapezoid, burnishing spatulas).
3. **Bespoke Contracting & Atelier (`/services/...`, `/atelier`, `/commission`):** Dedicated B2B commercial & residential applicator landing pages with online quote estimators and sample box order kits.
4. **Educational Field Guides (`/guides/...`):** Cost calculators ($/sq ft breakdown), troubleshooting guides, maintenance protocols, and direct material comparisons (*Microcement vs. Venetian Plaster*).
